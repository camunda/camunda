System partition scenarios

This page shows how the system partition changes its voters and passive members, scenario by scenario. Each step shows who starts it and the Raft configuration after it. The rules, the decisions and the rejected alternatives are in the design document system-partition-reconfiguration.md (revision 5). Section numbers in brackets point to it.
